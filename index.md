I am a 4th-year PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, specializing in network science and AI/ML applications for networks. I received my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Industrial Engineering from Boğaziçi University in Turkey.

My research focuses on applying network science to engineering systems, and more recently on machine learning on networks. With my advisor, Prof. Osman Yağan, I study the robustness of networks against overload-based (flow-based) cascading failures. Our modeling and analysis of such failures in multiplex networks is published in *IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering*, with follow-up work in the final editorial phase at *Physical Review E*. Our new work with Prof. Leman Akoglu on learning capacity allocations to improve robustness against cascading failures has been accepted to **KDD 2027**.

I am currently seeking summer internship opportunities in AI/ML, data science, and network science roles where I can apply my research skills to real-world problems. My technical expertise spans Python, machine learning (GNNs, reinforcement learning, LLMs, and recently diffusion models), network analysis, and simulation modeling.

Please feel free to explore my research, projects, and publications using the navigation above. I welcome inquiries about potential collaborations or internship opportunities.
